# Promo video sources

`promo-video.mp4` (62 s, 1920x1080) is built from real recordings of the app and plain graphics drawn in code. No AI-generated imagery or audio.

- **App footage:** screen recordings of Pantry Pal on the Android emulator, with made-up sample data (no real pantry data).
- **Graphics:** text and shapes drawn with ffmpeg/Pillow in the app's colours; the chef-hat icon is Phosphor "chef-hat" (MIT, see THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md).
- **Music:** "Happy Clappy Loop" by OwlishMedia, CC0 1.0 (public domain dedication, no attribution required): https://opengameart.org/content/happy-clappy-loop . Licence confirmed on the page when downloaded (16 November 2026).
- **Fonts:** Liberation Sans (SIL OFL 1.1).

YouTube upload notes for Play Console: the promo video must be a public or unlisted YouTube video, with ads/monetisation off and no age restriction. Even CC0 music can occasionally trigger an automatic Content ID match; if that happens, dispute it citing the CC0 licence above or swap the track.
